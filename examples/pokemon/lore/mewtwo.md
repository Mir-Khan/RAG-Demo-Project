# Mewtwo

Mewtwo is a Psychic-type Legendary Pokémon created by scientists through years of
genetic manipulation using DNA recovered from Mew. Although its genetic makeup is
nearly identical to Mew's, its temperament and abilities are radically different.

## Origin

Mewtwo was engineered in a laboratory on Cinnabar Island. Research journals
describe a project to clone Mew; the resulting creature was far more powerful and
aggressive than anticipated, and eventually destroyed the lab.

## Mega Evolution

Mewtwo is unusual in having two distinct Mega forms: Mega Mewtwo X, a
Psychic/Fighting attacker with enormous physical Attack, and Mega Mewtwo Y, a
pure Psychic form with an extreme Special Attack stat and a lighter frame.
