## The dominant core: Rillaboom + Sneasler

The single most common pairing in the format, appearing together on roughly
26% of teams. The idea:

- **Rillaboom** sets Grassy Terrain (Grassy Surge), which heals grounded
  Pokémon each turn and boosts Grass-type moves — a strong passive
  sustain-and-power tool in doubles, where both of a trainer's active
  Pokémon benefit from the terrain at once.
- **Sneasler** is a fast, hard-hitting Fighting/Poison attacker that
  capitalizes on the tempo Rillaboom's terrain and redirection support create,
  pressuring the opposing back line before it can set up.

This is a "grass-terrain-and-pressure" archetype: control the field with
Rillaboom, then punch through with a fast attacker while the terrain is up.

## Common items and abilities

- **Held items:** Life Orb, Sitrus Berry, Focus Sash, Leftovers, Rocky Helmet
  are the most common — a spread of raw power (Life Orb), survivability
  (Sitrus Berry, Leftovers), guaranteed one turn of action (Focus Sash), and
  chip damage on contact (Rocky Helmet).
- **Prevalent abilities:** Grassy Surge (Rillaboom), Intimidate
  (Incineroar, Salamence), Defiant (Kingambit), Psychic Surge (Indeedee-F).
  Intimidate in particular is a doubles staple — it weakens both of the
  opposing Pokémon's Attack the moment it switches in, for free.
