# Regulation Set M-C (Pokémon Champions)

Regulation Set M-C is the ruleset active in Pokémon Champions from September 9
to December 2, 2026. It is the current competitive format.

## Format rules

- **Battle format:** Double Battle, 4v4. Each trainer registers a Team List of
  6 legal Pokémon and selects 4 to bring into each match.
- **Level:** every Pokémon is automatically adjusted to Level 50 for battle,
  with stats recalculated accordingly — a Level 100 Pokémon and a Level 50
  Pokémon enter a match on equal footing.
- **Species Clause:** a team cannot contain two Pokémon that share the same
  National Pokédex number. Regional variants (e.g. Galarian and standard
  forms) count as the same species for this rule.
- **Item Clause:** no two Pokémon on a team may hold the exact same item.
  166 items are legal in M-C, including staples like Choice Scarf, Life Orb,
  Focus Sash, and Leftovers, plus 6 newly authorized Mega Stones and 12 other
  items added this set: Air Balloon, Binding Band, Eject Button, Electric
  Seed, Grassy Seed, Misty Seed, Psychic Seed, Leek, Normal Gem, Red Card,
  Rocky Helmet, and Terrain Extender.
- **Timing:** 45 seconds per turn, 90-second team preview, ~20 minutes
  maximum game time.

## What's new in M-C

M-C introduced 36 new Pokémon to the legal pool (regional forms, gender
variants, and the Galar starters — Rillaboom, Cinderace, and Inteleon —
among them) and 6 new Mega Evolutions:

- Mega Absol Z
- Mega Garchomp Z
- Mega Lucario Z
- Mega Salamence
- Mega Golisopod
- Mega Baxcalibur

Mega Salamence, Mega Golisopod, and Mega Baxcalibur are considered the most
immediately impactful additions to the competitive metagame.
