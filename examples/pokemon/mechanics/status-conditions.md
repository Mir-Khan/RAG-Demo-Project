# Status Conditions

Status conditions are persistent effects that impair a Pokémon in battle. A
Pokémon can hold at most one non-volatile status at a time.

## Burn

Burn deals damage equal to 1/16 of the Pokémon's maximum HP at the end of each
turn. It also halves the damage dealt by the Pokémon's physical (Attack-based)
moves. Fire-type Pokémon cannot be burned. Burn is cured by items such as a
Rawst Berry or a Full Heal.

## Paralysis

A paralysed Pokémon has its Speed reduced to 50% of its normal value, and has a
25% chance of being unable to move on any given turn. Electric-type Pokémon
(in recent generations) are immune to paralysis. Cured by a Cheri Berry.

## Poison and Badly Poisoned

Regular poison deals 1/8 of maximum HP per turn. Badly poisoned (toxic) starts at
1/16 and increases by 1/16 each turn it remains in effect, resetting if the
Pokémon switches out. Poison- and Steel-type Pokémon cannot be poisoned by
ordinary means.
